About FarmDirect

FarmDirect is a farm-to-consumer online marketplace connecting verified local farmers in Zamboanga City with buyers who seek fresh, locally sourced agricultural products. The platform serves as a bridge between agricultural producers and consumers, promoting local trade and food security in the region.

FarmDirect offers the following services:

  • An online marketplace for fresh agricultural products from Zamboanga City farms
  • A verified farmer registration and application system
  • A buyer registration and product browsing experience
  • Order and delivery coordination between farmers and buyers
  • A secure messaging system between farmers and buyers

Buyer Responsibilities

As a registered Buyer on FarmDirect, you agree to the following:

  • Provide an accurate delivery address for all orders placed
  • Pay for orders in full before or upon delivery as agreed with the farmer
  • Treat farmers and platform staff with respect in all communications
  • Not place orders with no intention of completing the purchase
  • Report any product quality issues within 24 hours of delivery
  • Not misuse the review or rating system to unfairly harm a farmer's reputation

Buyers are the primary consumers of the platform. All purchases support local Zamboanga City farmers directly, and buyers are encouraged to engage responsibly with the agricultural community.

5
Farmer / Seller Verification

All users start as regular Buyers upon registration. To become a verified Farmer/Seller on FarmDirect, users must complete the Farmer Verification process, which includes:

  • Submission of an RSBSA Certificate (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)
  • Submission of a Barangay Certificate confirming residency and farmer status
  • Submission of a valid government-issued ID
  • Providing accurate farm location, farm name, and list of products

FarmDirect reserves the right to approve or reject any farmer application. Applications are reviewed within 3–5 business days. Submitting false, forged, or invalid documents will result in immediate and permanent account suspension.

✅ Once approved: Your account will be upgraded to a Farmer account, giving you access to the Seller Dashboard where you can list products, manage orders, and communicate with buyers.
