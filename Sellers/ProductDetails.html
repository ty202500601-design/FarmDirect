About This Product

Our Fresh Tomatoes are grown on the fertile fields of Tugbungan, Zamboanga City by Juan Dela Cruz and the Sunrise Farm family. Each tomato is hand-picked every morning to ensure they reach buyers at peak ripeness with the best flavor and nutritional value.

Grown without synthetic pesticides and harvested daily, these tomatoes are perfect for home cooking, carinderias, and restaurants.

🕐 Freshness Notice (visible to buyers): This batch was harvested March 27, 2026. Estimated expiry: April 3, 2026 (4 days left). Buyers are prompted to order soon.

Why Choose Our Tomatoes?

  • 🌿 100% Organic — No synthetic pesticides or fertilizers
  • 🌅 Harvested Daily — Picked fresh every morning before delivery
  • 📦 Carefully Packed — Padded packaging to prevent bruising
  • 🔬 Lab-Tested — Certified clean and safe for consumption
  • 🌱 Sustainably Grown — Rainwater irrigation, composting practices

Best Uses

Salads, pasta sauce, bruschetta, fresh salsa, grilling, and sandwiches.

💡 Storage Tip (shown to buyers): Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Once cut, refrigerate and consume within 2 days.
